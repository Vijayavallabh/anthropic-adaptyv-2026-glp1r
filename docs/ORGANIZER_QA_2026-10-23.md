## The one decision this surfaces: disulfide-stapled designs

- **Why it matters.** The only *demonstrated* de-novo class-B agonists are **constrained/pre-organized** peptides (EvoBind cyclic GLP1R EC50~32nM; RareFold cyclic+NCAA; 9J1P disulfide-scaffolded). A disulfide staple pre-organizes the backbone → the best-*motivated* (not provable) shot at the active-state-stabilizing rigidity agonism needs. §1 says agonism is the win-label and binding is still ranked, and §7 now permits disulfides → this is a legitimate, firewall-safe hedge.
- **What is NOT claimed.** No agonism score. Staple designs are gated on the same **measurable** 2-folder reach/engagement bar as every other design, folded as the real tagged construct, calibrated against GLP-1(7-36) (agonist) and exendin(9-39) (antagonist) controls in the same run. The agonism edge is a labeled *prior*, never a ranking number.
- **Caveats before deploying.** (a) §7 is from a synthesis, not a verbatim quote we verified — the backbone-vs-disulfide reconciliation is technically sound and consistent with the owner-relayed "only linear", but the owner should confirm the paired-disulfide allowance directly in Slack before we deploy the route. (b) Swapping any design into the adopted 20 is an **owner-gated composition change** — I prepare the evidence + recommendation; the owner decides. (c) Keep only **paired** Cys (verify the S–S actually forms in-fold); drop any design whose two Cys don't come together (e.g. dpbss_011, Cα–Cα~10Å). (d) molecule_class stays `single_chain` (one chain + intramolecular disulfide).

**Bottom line:** v10 (all-linear) is valid under *both* interpretations and needs no change. The disulfide route is an *optional upside play* pending (a) the 2-folder evidence (running) and (b) owner confirmation — not a forced swap.
