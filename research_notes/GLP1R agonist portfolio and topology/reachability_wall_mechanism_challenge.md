# Adversarial re-challenge of the two load-bearing reachability/topology assumptions (GLP1R de-novo agonist) + what's-new scan (as of 2026-10-15)

Scope: re-challenge (A) the "class-B deep 7TM pocket is unreachable de-novo / folded peptides floor ~5 Å → antagonist" wall, and (B) "orthosteric threading is the only agonism route for an extracellularly-applied de-novo single-chain peptide." Plus a post-2026-10-01 literature scan. Builds on `reports/De novo GPCR agonist design methods.md` (reachability tax, generator SOTA, MD filter), `reports/GLP1R agonism first principles levers.md` (two-state + ENM NO-GOs), `reports/GLP1R de novo agonist design.md`. Firewall invariant obeyed throughout: class-B agonism is unrankable in-silico; cAMP/CRE-SEAP is the only verdict. All in-silico claims marked [in-silico]; mechanisms-without-proof marked [HYP].

## Key Question A — Is the ~5 Å reachability wall real, or does a concrete 2025–2026 route close the last ~5 Å into the GLP1R pocket within our constraints (≤20 designs, ≤8 H100, ≤10 days, uv/conda, ODC-BY-clean)?

### Takeaway
The wall's *mechanism* as stated ("deep class-B pocket physically unreachable de-novo") is **overstated and partly false** — EvoBind's cyclic peptides already reached GLP1R functional activation de-novo (the one existence proof). But the *operational* wall holds: every named deep-insertion route is either class-A-only at a budget-incompatible hit rate, eligibility-blocked, or a generation tactic that only reshuffles binders the firewall says we still cannot rank for agonism. No named method delivers a concrete, firewall-compatible, ≤10-day lever that yields a confident, eligible, deep-reaching agonist. **The true binding constraints are unrankability + the ≤20-slot budget + linear/disulfide eligibility — not reachability per se.**

### Cited Findings
- **Baker/Skape targeted the class-B ECD by DESIGN CHOICE to MAKE antagonists — the deep-core-unreachable claim is NOT empirically tested by them.** For GLP1R/GIPR/GCGR/CGRPR they "targeted the soluble extracellular domain (ECD)," choosing ECD hotspots (e.g. GLP1R L95, GIPR M32, GCGR F33/W36/W87, CGRPR L33/W72/F92) specifically because ECD binding "induce[s] steric hindrance and prevent[s] peptide interaction" — i.e. antagonism was the goal. They did not attempt or test class-B deep-pocket insertion — [bioRxiv 2025.03.23.644666, 2025-03-23](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.03.23.644666v1.full); peer-reviewed [Nature 2026, 10.1038/s41586-026-10656-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10656-8) (PMID 42168559).
- **The only deep-insertion scaffolding method (Baker "penetrating pentamer") is class-A-only and budget-incompatible.** For class-A CXCR4: ~1,000 penetrating-pentamer motifs from hotspots W94/I259/I284 → 50 deepest scaffolded → ~1,000 scaffolds each (~50,000) → 26,000 screened by biopanning → **2 hits, both from one backbone (~0.008%)**; the lead dCX1_001 (IC50 24 nM) is an **antagonist**. No statement that this transfers to class B — [bioRxiv 2025.03.23.644666](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.03.23.644666v1.full).
- **De-novo class-B GLP1R agonism HAS been achieved — with CYCLIC peptides (the one precedent).** EvoBind2 (AlphaFold2/-Multimer, cyclic offset) designed 15/18/31-mer head-to-tail **backbone-cyclized** peptides; the **18-mer reached EC50 31.9 nM at GLP1R, 3/3 tested activated** cAMP (15-mer 11.8 µM, 31-mer 5.0 µM; Liraglutide 2.4 nM ref), **G-protein biased (no β-arrestin)**, sequence-novel ("almost no consecutive sequence similarity" vs PDB 8JIQ GLP-1 analogue). In-silico funnel: 33 lengths × 5 seeds × 5,000 iters = 825,000 steps → top 10% → 20,387 at plDDT≥85 → AFM → 5 selected → 3 synthesized (Berzelius; no GPU-hrs given) — [bioRxiv 2025.06.06.658268, 2025-06-06](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.06.06.658268v1.full).
- **Critically, EvoBind never modeled the peptide–GLP1R complex** — insertion depth and activation-contact engagement at GLP1R are UNREPORTED (they modeled only GCGR, where designs "go down into the binding pocket regardless of length"). The authors state agonism for class B is "particularly challenging… ligand-binding sites typically accommodate extended linear peptide conformations" and cyclic peptides "engage less receptor surface" — [bioRxiv 2025.06.06.658268](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.06.06.658268v1.full).
- **RFpeptides (macrocycles) is demonstrated only on soluble protein SURFACES, never a deep/membrane pocket, and is head-to-tail cyclic.** ≤20 designs/target across GABARAP (KD 6 nM), MCL1 & MDM2 (1–10 µM), RbtA (<10 nM, from predicted structure); X-ray RMSD <1.5 Å (NChemBiol) / <2 Å (preprint); PDB 9CDT/9CDU/9CDV/9HGC/9HGD. Cyclic relative positional encoding joins N/C termini by a peptide bond — [Nat Chem Biol 2025; bioRxiv 2024.11.18.622547](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.11.18.622547.full.pdf); [Rosetta Commons 2025-06-26](https://rosettacommons.org/2025/06/26/introducing-deep-learning-designed-macrocycles-high-affinity-protein-binders).
- **RFdiffusion3's buried-atom control conditions on how buried the LIGAND is in the DESIGNED protein (inverse topology) — i.e. for building a pocket AROUND a ligand, not for threading a chain INTO a fixed receptor pocket.** All-atom, open-source; RASA labels (buried/partial/exposed) + H-bond donor/acceptor + COM conditioning. No GPCR/membrane-pocket benchmark — [bioRxiv 2025.09.18.676967 / PMC12458353](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12458353/); Foundry release [2025-12-22](https://rosettacommons.org/2025/12/22/rfdiffusion3-is-now-available-in-foundry/); also Neurosnap/Tamarind.
- **ODesign flexible-target mode** "consistently surpassed" rigid mode and beat RFdiffusionAA 2.38× / BoltzDesign1 1,142× on ligand-pocket design — but no class-B GPCR deep-insertion demonstration; license not confirmed first-hand — [arXiv 2510.22304, Oct 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.22304v1).
- **No standardized "reachability/insertion-depth" benchmark exists; the depth-selection step is bespoke.** Model fidelity is not the bottleneck (deep class-A binders matched cryo-EM to ~0.7–1.0 Å); yield is — [reports/De novo GPCR agonist design methods.md].

### Inferences
- **GO/NO-GO per named route** (all firewall-blocked as agonism levers; compute on ≤8 H100):
  - **Penetrating-pentamer → class B: NO-GO.** Compute-feasible (~3–6 days for ~1k motifs + ~50k scaffolds + MPNN + AF2 filter), but class-A yield 0.008% means a confident hit needs ~10⁴ screened designs vs our ≤20 slots; and the method produced *antagonists*. Even a deep inserter can't be ranked for TM6-drive [in-silico]. Budget math alone kills it.
  - **Pocket-seeded partial diffusion (RFdiffusion/DiffPepBuilder on 6X18): NO-GO as a wall-breaker** (marginal as diversity). Feasible (~2–5 days, few-hundred designs). But (i) firewall: depth is a necessary-condition filter, not an agonism ranker [in-silico]; (ii) seeding a deep pose on the native "message" geometry crosses the His7/eligibility red line; a de-novo deep pose carries no agonism guarantee. It only reshuffles unrankable binders — v5b already has pocket-threaders from 3 methods.
  - **RFdiffusion3 all-atom pocket-threader: NO-GO as wall-breaker** (its all-atom *target*-side-chain modelling is a genuine orthogonal plus over backbone-only methods, cf. the project lesson "af2ig needs a full-atom target"). Feasible (~2–5 days). But the buried-atom control is inverse-topology, there is no class-B demo, and the firewall still blocks ranking.
  - **ODesign flexible mode: NO-GO as wall-breaker** (flexible-target induced-fit modelling is theoretically apt for a soft class-B pocket). Feasible compute; license unverified; no class-B demo; firewall unchanged.
  - **RFpeptides macrocycles: NO-GO under current eligibility** (head-to-tail cyclic ≠ "linear/disulfide"); and no deep-pocket capability shown. Conditional only as a *second* cyclic generator IF the owner rules head-to-tail macrocycles eligible.
- **The stated 5 Å "floor → antagonist" is itself an in-silico inference about OUR designs that the firewall forbids trusting as an efficacy call** [in-silico]. EvoBind's n=3 proof — whose GLP1R pose was never even modeled — shows de-novo peptides *can* functionally engage the class-B pocket without us being able to measure or rank their depth. So "5 Å = antagonist-leaning" is not a demonstrated verdict; only the assay decides.
- **The sharpest, actionable nuance: the sole existence proof is CYCLIC; v5b's eligible designs are presumably linear — and linear de-novo GLP1R agonism has NO precedent** [HYP]. Mechanism: class-B pockets bind *extended* conformations and cyclic peptides engage less surface (per EvoBind), so a floppy linear de-novo peptide may neither reach nor hold the activating geometry that the cyclic 18-mer achieved. This is an owner-level eligibility decision, not an in-silico lever: if cyclic/disulfide is permitted, reproducing the proven cyclic format — or **disulfide-stapled** pocket-threaders (eligible; DiffPepBuilder-MIT and EvoBind2 both add disulfides; cf. side-chain-cyclized GLP-1R agonists at nM, J Med Chem 2015) — is a higher-prior, firewall-compatible, ≤10-day *generation* hedge than more floppy-linear bets.

### Gaps
- EvoBind's actual GLP1R insertion depth / activation-contact map is unpublished (complex not modeled), so the depth required for class-B agonism is unquantified in the one precedent.
- Baker never ran penetrating-pentamer on class B, so its class-B deep-insertion yield is unmeasured (inferred futile from class-A 0.008% + antagonist outcome).
- ODesign license not verified first-hand (ODC-BY-clean status unknown for submitted designs).

## Key Question B — Is there ANY extracellularly-accessible NON-orthosteric agonism mechanism reachable by a de-novo single-chain peptide (ECD conformational-selection; exoframe/membrane-proximal; extracellular ago-PAM)?

### Takeaway
**No. Assumption B holds firmly.** Every non-orthosteric route is closed: ECD-surface engagement is a *directly demonstrated* antagonist; the genuinely new de-novo "exoframe" agonism mechanism (GEMs, Nature 2026) binds the **lipid-facing TM surface** and is a membrane-embedded protein inaccessible to an extracellularly-applied soluble peptide (and class-A only); and **every known GLP1R allosteric agonist/PAM site is either intracellular (compound 2/BETP at C347) or a pure PAM with no intrinsic efficacy at a membrane-embedded site (LSN3160440).** Peptide ago-PAM is CLOSED. Orthosteric threading remains the only route for an extracellular de-novo single-chain peptide.

### Cited Findings
- **ECD-surface binding is a DEMONSTRATED antagonist, not an agonist.** Baker's de-novo GLP1R/GIPR/GCGR ECD binders are antagonists (CGRPR confirmed competitive antagonists in cAMP, e.g. dC2_049 IC50 4.5 nM); ECD engagement was chosen *because* it sterically blocks the native peptide — [bioRxiv 2025.03.23.644666](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.03.23.644666v1.full).
- **GLP1R ECD does show a closed↔open equilibrium shifted open by peptide, but ECD opening alone is not activation.** Apo favors closed; peptide "trigger[s] the transition… to the otherwise energetically unfavourable open conformation"; 1.6 µs MD: ECD closed when bound, open when unbound; activation funnels into a shared change at the **extracellular end of TM6** that enables G-protein coupling — i.e. the N-terminal "message" + TM6 drive are required — [PMC7062719](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7062719); [conformational-transitions MD, PMC12348053](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12348053/). Conformational-selection is an interpretation of closed→open data, not a kinetically proven mechanism.
- **De-novo "GPCR exoframe modulators" (GEMs) can be ago-PAMs — but bind the lipid-facing TM surface and are class-A only.** De-novo proteins "that specifically target the transmembrane domain," inspired by "natural regulation of GPCRs by transmembrane proteins"; the **ago-PAM GEM restores activity of D1 receptor loss-of-function mutants** (intrinsic activating capability); cryo-EM D1R–Gs with ago-PAM GEM targeting **TM5/6/7** (PDB 9LLH, 2.73 Å; NAM GEM 9LLG, 2.77 Å). Tested only on **dopamine D1 (class A)** — [Nature 2026, 10.1038/s41586-025-09957-1, pub 2026-02-16](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09957-1) (PMID 41699180); [ZJU release 2026-02-27](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/2026/0227/c19573a3135945/page.htm).
- **GLP1R ago-PAMs compound 2 and BETP bind the INTRACELLULAR end of TM6 (covalent to C347, cytoplasmic side).** Compound 2 is a covalent ago-PAM (intrinsic partial agonism ~80% Emax cAMP) triggering TM6 outward movement "in cooperation with the ECD"; electrophilic/poor PK — [Nat Commun 2021, PMID 34145245](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8213797/); [bioRxiv 2021.05.29.446269](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2021.05.29.446269.full.pdf); PDB 7DUQ/7E14.
- **LSN3160440 is a pure PAM (molecular glue) requiring an orthosteric peptide — NO intrinsic agonism — bound high in the TM bundle at the TM1/TM2 interface.** "Stabilizes an active state… by cooperatively binding both receptor and orthosteric ligand"; strongly probe-dependent — [Nat Chem Biol 2020, Bueno et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s41589-020-0589-7); PDB 6VCB (3.30 Å), EMDB-21147.

### Inferences
- **Exoframe (GEM) route is CLOSED for this challenge by delivery/format, not by nonexistence** [HYP + sourced]. Mechanism: the ago-PAM GEM site is the lipid-facing TM5/6/7 surface; reaching it requires the modulator to partition into / span the bilayer (like natural single-TM regulators such as RAMP/MRAP). An extracellularly-applied soluble natural-AA single-chain peptide in a whole-cell cAMP/SEAP assay cannot access a lipid-embedded surface, and a membrane-spanning design is not a soluble peptide (expression/folding/delivery fail the format). Also class-A only. This is the single most interesting "real-but-out-of-format" finding — a genuine de-novo TM-targeting agonism mechanism that the Adaptyv delivery format excludes.
- **Peptide ago-PAM is CLOSED** [sourced]. The two sites with *intrinsic* agonism (compound 2, BETP) are intracellular (C347) — unreachable by an extracellular peptide. The one more-extracellular allosteric site (LSN3160440, TM1/2) is a *pure PAM* with no intrinsic efficacy and would score negative in an agonist assay lacking an orthosteric agonist; it is also inside the membrane-embedded TM bundle. No extracellularly-accessible GLP1R site with intrinsic agonist efficacy for a peptide is known.
- **ECD-only conformational-selection agonism is firewall-closed** [HYP, mechanism]. Stabilizing the open-ECD state does not transmit the TM6 kink: the ECD is a discrete domain joined to the TMD by a flexible stalk, and class-B activation requires the peptide N-terminal message *inside* the TMD to drive TM6 at PxxG. Baker's ECD binders → antagonists is the direct empirical confirmation. This reduces to the already-DOWNGRADED two-domain idea and to "the ECD is the antagonist site."

### Gaps
- No experiment tests whether a soluble de-novo protein could engage the GLP1R TM1/2 extracellular-vestibule rim with *intrinsic* efficacy (LSN site is PAM-only; no agonist analog reported). This is an untested-but-very-low-prior corner, not a lever.

## Key Question C — What's new since 2026-10-14 (prefer primary sources after 2026-10-01)?

### Takeaway
**Nothing new moves the wall.** The post-2026-10-01 bioRxiv scan (bioengineering/biophysics/bioinformatics) and arXiv/PubMed checks surface only adjacent tooling (binder-campaign orchestration, co-folding generalization, a sequence-classifier provenance critique) — no new de-novo GPCR/class-B agonist, no new deep-pocket insertion method, and no new agonism predictor. EvoBind (2025-06) remains the sole direct class-B GLP1R-agonist precedent.

### Cited Findings
- **"Agentic campaign control for high-throughput de novo binder design"** — LLM-agent orchestration across binder-design tools; pipeline automation, not a new generator or GPCR result — [bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.09.22.753604, v3 2026-10-05].
- **"Towards Generalizable Protein–ligand Co-folding with ACER"** — co-folders (AF3-class) "fail to generalize"; reinforces the firewall that co-fold confidence is unreliable off-distribution (class-B/novel) — [bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.06.02.728568, v2 2026-10-01].
- **"Quantifying the Provenance-to-Function Gap in Antidiabetic Peptide Prediction"** — homology-aware evaluation shows sequence-based antidiabetic-peptide classifiers lose accuracy out-of-distribution; reinforces that function cannot be read from sequence — [bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.09.25.754557, 2026-10-01].
- **Proteína-Complexa** — fully atomistic binder generator with generative pretraining + test-time compute (Teddymer dataset); higher in-silico success, general binder design, not GPCR/agonism; code "to be released" — [arXiv 2603.27950, March 2026](https://alphaxiv.org/abs/2603.27950). (Newest generator advance found; predates the Oct window and does not address agonism.)
- **NOAH-ARK1 universal GPCR structure-determination pipeline** — de-novo fiducial + AI construct screening for cryo-EM; a structure-biology tool, not design — [Nat Struct Mol Biol 2026, 10.1038/s41594-026-01869-6, pub 2026-08-28](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41594-026-01869-6) (PMID 42665663).
- **"AI-driven de-novo ultra-long-acting GLP-1RAs"** — 10,000 designed → 60 passed → 52% in-vitro hit; D13 ≈ semaglutide weight loss; ~2-week design cycle. Almost certainly GLP-1-scaffold analogs (half-life/efficacy framing vs semaglutide), i.e. likely NOT sequence-novel de-novo → **eligibility caveat** — [Adv Sci 2025, PMC12561408](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12561408/); [bioRxiv 2025.03.26.645438](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.03.26.645438.full.pdf).
- bioRxiv new-DOI prefix `10.64898/` is in effect for 2026 postings (noted so the report-writer resolves links correctly).

### Inferences
- The 2025 EvoBind preprint's own framing — a successful GLP1R/GCGR agonist must "(1) bind with sufficient affinity, (2) fit within the confined activation pocket, (3) induce receptor activation through conformational rearrangement of the transmembrane domain" — is the field's current consensus and exactly restates the orthosteric-threading + TM6-drive requirement [sourced]. Nothing post-2026-10-01 contradicts it.

## VERDICT

### Takeaway
**The wall holds. Reconfirm v5b (diversity-of-clean-shots).** Neither assumption's challenges open a concrete, firewall-compatible, ≤10-day lever that would change the submission. Assumption A's *mechanism* is overstated (EvoBind proves de-novo cyclic peptides reach class-B activation), but its *operational* conclusion survives; Assumption B holds firmly (all non-orthosteric routes closed).

### Decisive calls
- **No deep-insertion generation run is worth idle-GPU time as a wall-breaker.** Penetrating-pentamer (class-A, 0.008% yield, antagonist) is budget-incompatible; pocket-seeded partial diffusion / RFdiffusion3 / ODesign are compute-feasible (~2–5 days on ≤8 H100) but only reshuffle binders the firewall forbids ranking, and partial-diffusion-on-native-geometry risks the His7 eligibility red line. [in-silico] depth is not an agonism predictor.
- **Peptide ago-PAM and ECD-only/exoframe agonism are CLOSED** (intracellular sites, pure-PAM no-efficacy, or lipid-embedded/membrane-protein format inaccessible to an extracellular soluble peptide; ECD surface = demonstrated antagonist). Orthosteric threading is the only accessible route.
- **Nothing new since 2026-10-14 changes strategy.**
- **One owner-level item worth surfacing (eligibility, not an in-silico lever):** the sole existence proof (EvoBind) is **head-to-tail cyclic**; v5b's eligible designs are presumably linear, and **linear de-novo GLP1R agonism has no precedent** [HYP]. If eligibility permits cyclic or (eligible) disulfide-stapled peptides, a cheap, firewall-compatible hedge is to prioritize the proven cyclic format or **disulfide-stapled pocket-threaders** (DiffPepBuilder-MIT / EvoBind2 add disulfides) over floppy linear bets — higher mechanistic prior for reaching and holding the confined class-B activation pocket, at no cost to the ≤20-slot diversity logic.
- The genuine new finding (GEM de-novo exoframe ago-PAM, Nature 2026) is **real but out-of-format** — a de-novo TM-targeting agonism mechanism the Adaptyv extracellular-application assay excludes; worth logging to the NO-GO ledger as "exoframe: closed by delivery format + class-A only," not as a lever.

Never claim a proven agonist. The CRE-SEAP amplification remains the quiet ally: a partial agonist that merely nudges TM6 can read positive, which is the realistic shape of a first-of-its-kind class-B result at this frontier.
